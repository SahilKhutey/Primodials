## Short Description
Polygonal Primordials is a living wallpaper that brings an evolving ecosystem to your desktop. Polygonal creatures with heritable traits drift, hunt, and reproduce in real-time. Choose from atmospheric themes, customize the experience, or join the Steam Workshop community.
