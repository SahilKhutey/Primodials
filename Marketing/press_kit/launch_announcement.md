# 🎉 Polygonal Primordials Live Wallpaper — Now Available on Steam!

**Posted:** Q1 2027

After over a year of development, **Polygonal Primordials Live Wallpaper** is finally here! It's a living, evolving ecosystem for your desktop that doubles as a meditation on digital life.

## What's Polygonal Primordials?
Imagine looking at your desktop and seeing something beautiful and alive — not a static image, but a real simulation of creatures with heritable traits, evolving and competing in real-time. Built on a custom C++23 game engine (Shape Engine), the wallpaper runs at 30 FPS using less than 2% CPU.
