**Posted:** August 3, 2026

## What is it?

Polygonal Primordials is a data-oriented simulation engine and game that lets you watch digital life evolve. Polygonal creatures with heritable traits live, hunt, reproduce, and evolve in a deterministic ecosystem.

Built from scratch in modern C++23, it demonstrates a production-grade archetype-based ECS capable of simulating 250,000+ entities at 60 FPS.
