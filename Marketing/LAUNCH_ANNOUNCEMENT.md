# 🎉 Polygonal Primordials is LIVE!

We are excited to announce the release of **Polygonal Primordials v2.0.0** — an ecosystem simulation and live wallpaper engine built from scratch in C++23.

## 🌟 What's Included in v2.0.0

- **Real-Time Genetic Algorithms**: Watch creatures evolve speed, size, vision, and aggression over hours of simulation.
- **High-Performance ECS Engine**: 250,000 entities @ 60 FPS.
- **Live Desktop Wallpaper Mode**: Transform your desktop background into a living, breathing ecosystem.
- **Steam Workshop & Multi-Language**: Community theme publishing + 11 localized languages.
