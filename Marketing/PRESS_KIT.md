## Long Description (200 words)

Polygonal Primordials is a data-oriented simulation engine packaged as a beautiful, accessible game. Built from scratch in modern C++23, the Shape Engine v2.0 demonstrates the power of data-oriented design with a custom archetype-based Entity Component System capable of simulating 250,000+ entities at 60 FPS.

The flagship game is a Lotka-Volterra artificial life sandbox: polygonal creatures with heritable traits (speed, size, vision, aggression) live, hunt, reproduce, and evolve over time. The simulation is fully deterministic — given the same seed, every run produces identical results.

What sets Polygonal Primordials apart:
- **Real evolution**: 8 heritable traits per creature with mutation, crossover, and natural selection.
- **High performance**: Custom archetype-based ECS, spatial hash broadphase, OpenGL instanced rendering.
- **Open source**: Engine under MIT license — fork it, learn from it, build your own.
- **Cross-platform**: Identical experience on Windows, Linux, and macOS.
- **Multi-language**: 11 languages supported out of the box.
