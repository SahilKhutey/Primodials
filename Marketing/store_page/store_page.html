Polygonal Primordials

A Living Wallpaper for Your Desktop

Polygonal Primordials is not a static wallpaper. It's a real simulation of polygonal creatures with heritable traits, running on your desktop while you work.

Watch them drift, hunt, reproduce, and evolve. See species emerge and go extinct. Read their story in the Ecosystem Diary.

It's a nature documentary you live inside.

🌊 Five Atmospheric Themes Included

  • Deep Sea Bioluminescence — Glowing creatures drift through dark ocean depths
  • Coral Reef — Tropical colors pulse through shallow waters
  • Aurora — Translucent lights drift across a polar sky
  • Microorganisms — Microscopic view of pulsing microbes
  • Forest Floor — Dappled light through mossy ground

⚡ Power Efficient

  • 30 FPS default (configurable to 15 or 60)
  • <2% CPU usage
  • Auto-pauses when fullscreen app takes over
  • Adaptive quality on laptop battery
