# Polygonal Primordials

A living wallpaper for your desktop. Real ecosystems, evolving in real-time.

## 🌊 What Is It?
Polygonal Primordials is not a static wallpaper. It's a **real simulation** of polygonal creatures with heritable traits, running on your desktop while you work. Watch them drift, hunt, reproduce, and evolve. See species emerge and go extinct. Read their story in the Ecosystem Diary. It's a nature documentary you live inside.
