## 3. SHORT DESCRIPTION (~280 chars)

> Polygonal Primordials is a real-time artificial ecosystem that runs on your desktop. Six biomes from peaceful ponds to volcanic vents. Watch predator-prey dynamics, population cycles and emergent behavior. Free, open source, MIT licensed. By a solo developer in Bilaspur, India.

---

## 4. LONG DESCRIPTION

> Polygonal Primordials turns any monitor into a window into a tiny artificial ecosystem. Inspired by classical predator-prey mathematics (Lotka-Volterra), every organism follows real ecological rules: prey graze, predators hunt, populations rise and fall.
>
> Six default biomes included: Algae Bloom (peaceful pond), Deep Sea (bioluminescent abyss), Primordial Soup (4 billion years ago), Tundra (boom-bust cycles), Coral Reef (peak diversity), Volcanic Vent (extremophiles).
>
> Use it as a live wallpaper, screensaver-style display, or interactive simulation. Adjust speed, pause to inspect, watch generations evolve.
>
> Built by Sahil Khutey (Gensouls Lab) as a solo project over [X months]. Released under MIT license. Free, with no ads, no telemetry, no DRM.
