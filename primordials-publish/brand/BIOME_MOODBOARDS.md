## BIOME 01 — ALGAE BLOOM (Default)
**Pitch:** *"A teeming pond surface at dawn — green algae flourish, daphnia graze, copepods hunt."*  
**Palette Extension:**
- Algae Green: `#4ECDC4`
- Deep Teal: `#1B998B`
- Pollen Yellow: `#FFE66D`

**Image Prompt:**
> *"Top-down view of a pond surface at dawn, abstract polygonal organisms: hundreds of small green hexagons (algae), 30 larger translucent circles (daphnia), 8 sharp triangles (copepods) gliding. Deep teal water, algae green blooms, soft yellow pollen specks. Flat vector, polygonal art, low-poly, minimalist, scientific illustration, soft glow. Wide aspect ratio 16:9."*
