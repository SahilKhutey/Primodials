🧬 **PRIMORDIALS — A LIVING ECOSYSTEM ON YOUR DESKTOP**

Primordials is a real-time artificial life simulation that turns your monitor into a window into a primordial world. Inspired by the classic Lotka-Volterra predator-prey equations, every organism you see follows real ecological rules.

Built solo in C++23 + TypeScript over 6 months in Bilaspur, India. Open source under MIT.
