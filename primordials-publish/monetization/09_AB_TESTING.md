# 🧪 PRIMODIALS — A/B Testing Framework

## 🎯 KEY BACKLOG OF EXPERIMENTS

### Test 001: Hero CTA Copy
- **Hypothesis:** "Try Primordials" converts higher than "Download Free".
- **Variants:**
  - A (Control): "Download Free"
  - B: "Get Started"
  - C: "Try Primordials"
- **Primary Metric:** Landing page to installer download CTR.

### Test 002: Demo Visual Asset
- **Hypothesis:** 6-second high-framerate looping GIF converts higher than a 30-second embedded video.
- **Primary Metric:** Click-through to `/download`.

### Test 003: Pricing Display
- **Hypothesis:** Showing only Free + Lifetime converts higher than showing full multi-tier subscriptions on the initial storefront visit.
- **Primary Metric:** Conversion to any paid tier.
