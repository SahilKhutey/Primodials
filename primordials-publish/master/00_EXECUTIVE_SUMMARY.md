# 🧬 PRIMODIALS — Executive Summary

> **A living ecosystem on your desktop.**  
> Real Lotka-Volterra predator-prey dynamics. Open source. MIT licensed.  
> Built solo in Bilaspur, India.  

---

## 🎯 THE PRODUCT

Primordials is a desktop-native artificial life simulation that runs as a live wallpaper, screensaver, or windowed app. Every organism follows real ecological rules — populations rise and fall according to the same differential math taught in biology and ecology courses since the 1920s.

**Why anyone cares:** Screensavers and desktop backgrounds have been dead or looping video files for 30 years. Primordials is the first one that is genuinely alive.
