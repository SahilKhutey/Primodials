## 🎯 DAILY OPERATIONAL RHYTHM (30–45 min total)

### 1. Morning Standup (9:00 AM EST — 15 min)
- Review Plausible analytics (24h unique visits, referrer breakdown).
- Triage GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Review Discord `#mod-log` and check for pending onboarding queries.
- Post morning update or community question in Discord `#general`.

### 2. Midday Pulse (12:00 PM EST — 15 min)
- Reply to social comments across Twitter/X, Reddit, and Product Hunt.
- Welcome new community members in Discord `#introductions`.
- Verify scheduled posts in Buffer / Hootsuite.

### 3. Evening Wrap (6:00 PM EST — 15 min)
- Record daily metrics in [`master/07_KPI_DASHBOARD.md`](file:///c:/Users/ASUS/Documents/Primodilas/primordials-publish/master/07_KPI_DASHBOARD.md).
- Identify top 3 priorities for tomorrow.
