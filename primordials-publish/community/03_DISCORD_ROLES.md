## 🤖 AUTOMATED ROLE TRANSITIONS

- **On Join:** User is assigned `🌱 Newcomer`.
- **Promotion to `🧬 Member`:** Triggers automatically when an account reaches **7 days in server** AND posts **5+ non-spam messages** in community channels.
- **Contributor Roles:** Auto-assigned or synchronized via Discord Bot when PRs merge on GitHub.
