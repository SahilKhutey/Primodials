# 🧬 PRIMODIALS — Community Architecture Brief

**Mission:** Build a calm, curious, and scientifically wonder-filled community around Primordials — a place where ecosystem enthusiasts, systems programmers, and generative artists help each other build living desktop environments.

**Core Values:** Patient · Curious · Grounded · Welcoming  
**Tone:** Scientific wonder, zero corporate jargon, zero hype. Like an open ecology laboratory.  

---

## 🎯 KEY YEAR 1 OBJECTIVES

1. **Discord Community:** 500+ active members participating across biome creation and engine modding.
2. **GitHub Discussions:** 100+ high-signal technical threads and biome proposals.
3. **Contributor Retention:** 30%+ of first-time biome creators submitting secondary biomes.
4. **Support Response Time:** Under 12 hours for technical and setup troubleshooting.
5. **Community Sentiment:** 95%+ positive, welcoming atmosphere with zero tolerance for toxicity.

---

## 🏛️ FOUR COMMUNITY PILLARS

1. **Showcase & Terrariums:** Show off customized desktop wallpapers, monitor setups, and custom JSON parameters.
2. **Technical Support & Onboarding:** Frictionless help with installations, multi-monitor configurations, and Linux/macOS builds.
3. **Engine & Simulation Research:** Deep discussions on ECS data orientation, Lotka-Volterra stability, and WebGPU roadmap items.
4. **Natural History & Ecological Science:** Sharing real-world marine biology, microbial ecology papers, and mathematical biology findings.
