## 🏗️ SERVER CATEGORY TREE

```
🧬 Primordials — Living Ecosystem
│
├── 📜 INFORMATION
│   ├── #welcome            (Automated onboarding & quick links)
│   ├── #rules              (Code of Conduct summary)
│   ├── #announcements      (Releases, devlogs, major events)
│   └── #changelog          (Automated GitHub release mirror)
│
├── 💬 COMMUNITY
│   ├── #general            (Ecosystem chat & discussions)
│   ├── #introductions      (New member greetings & setups)
│   ├── #show-and-tell      (Custom biomes, forks, mods)
│   ├── #screenshots        (Desktop wallpapers & setups)
│   └── #off-topic          (Casual chill chat)
│
├── 🛠️ SUPPORT
│   ├── #help               (Installation & configuration)
│   ├── #bug-reports        (Defect triage & GitHub issue linking)
│   ├── #feature-requests   (Community ideas)
│   └── #faq                (Pinned troubleshooting guides)
│
├── 🌿 BIOMES
│   ├── #biome-discussion   (Tuning & balancing parameters)
│   ├── #biome-showcase     (WIP biome clips & JSON snippets)
│   ├── #biome-requests     (Ideas for upcoming ecosystems)
│   └── #biome-workshop     (Forum channel for collaborative design)
│
├── 💻 DEVELOPMENT
│   ├── #dev-general        (C++23 & React architecture)
│   ├── #engine-discussion  (ECS, SIMD, and SDL3 rendering)
│   ├── #prs-and-issues     (GitHub webhook stream)
│   └── #dev-random         (Algorithms & technical memes)
│
├── 🧪 ECOLOGY & SCIENCE
│   ├── #real-ecology       (Marine biology & food webs)
│   ├── #math-and-physics   (Differential equations & cellular automata)
│   └── #cool-papers        (Research links & academic papers)
│
└── 🔒 STAFF
    ├── #mod-chat           (Internal moderation coordination)
    ├── #mod-log            (Automated audit log stream)
    └── #admin-only         (Maintainer records)
```

## ⚙️ SERVER SECURITY & BRANDING

- **Server Icon:** Primary vector brand mark (`assets/logo/logo-icon.svg`).
- **Server Banner:** Abyssal Teal gradient with glowing magenta accent.
- **Verification Level:** Low (Must have verified email on Discord account).
- **Default Notifications:** Mentions Only (Reduces notification fatigue).
- **2FA Requirement:** Enabled for all Moderator and Admin roles.
