# 📜 PRIMODIALS — Community Guidelines & Culture

> *"A quiet, curious terrarium for people who love mathematics, biology, and alive computing."*

---

## 🌟 CORE CONDUCT PRINCIPLES

1. **Be Welcoming to Learners:** Whether someone is asking how to install Wallpaper Engine or debugging C++ template metaprogramming, answer with kindness and clarity.
2. **Constructive Technical Discourse:** Critique systems and code, never individuals. Use data, benchmarks, and reproduction steps.
3. **No Hype or Speculation:** We focus on real computational biology, creative modding, and enjoyable desktop experiences. Avoid cryptocurrency, NFT, or commercial hype.
4. **No Spam or Self-Promotion:** Keep discussions relevant to ecosystem simulation, graphics programming, generative art, and biology.

---

## 🛡️ INCIDENT ESCALATION LADDER

* **Level 1 — Gentle DM Reminder:** Private message explaining community expectations.
* **Level 2 — Formal Warning:** Mod log entry + 1-hour channel timeout.
* **Level 3 — 24-Hour Timeout:** Temporary cooling-off period.
* **Level 4 — Server Kick:** Removed from server (re-entry permitted after review).
* **Level 5 — Permanent Ban:** Immediate revocation for hate speech, harassment, or malicious behavior.
