## 🎯 COMMUNITY HEALTH THRESHOLDS

| Indicator | Optimal State ✅ | Warning State ⚠️ | Critical Action Required 🚨 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Moderator Actions** | < 5 per week | 6–15 per week | > 15 per week (Spam attack / Raid) |
| **Member Retention (30d)** | > 50% active | 25%–50% active | < 25% (Rethink onboarding flow) |
| **Biome Submissions** | 2+ per month | 1 per month | 0 in 60 days (Run a Biome Jam event) |
| **Q&A Response Time** | < 6 hours | 6–24 hours | > 48 hours (Enlist more community helpers) |
