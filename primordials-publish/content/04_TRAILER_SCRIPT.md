## 📜 3-ACT STRUCTURE

* **0.0s – 5.0s:** Complete darkness. A lone circular cell appears in the deep void, glowing softly in cyan (`#4ECDC4`).
* **5.0s – 10.0s:** Additional cells bloom and divide. Ambient music builds with quiet anticipation.
* **10.0s – 15.0s:** A sharp, magenta triangular predator (`#FF4FA3`) darts from the shadows, initiating pursuit.
* **15.0s – 20.0s:** Sudden impact/capture. Hard cut to black.

### ACT 2: THE SHOWCASE (20.0s – 65.0s)
* **20.0s – 38.0s:** Full exploration of all 6 biomes in 3-second cinematic sweeps:
  1. *Algae Bloom:* Teeming pond at dawn.

  3. *Primordial Soup:* Ancient volcanic ocean chemistry.
  4. *Tundra:* Brutal and beautiful Arctic boom-bust cycles.
  5. *Coral Reef:* High-density tropical biodiversity.
  6. *Volcanic Vent:* Extremophiles thriving on sulfur beds.
* **38.0s – 50.0s:** Pull-back to show mathematical ecology in action.
  * **Text Overlay:** *"Real Lotka-Volterra differential equations. Continuous evolutionary genetics."*
* **50.0s – 65.0s:** Technical and interactive showcase:
  * 60 FPS counter at 250,000 entities.
  * Modifying species parameters, food generation, and gravity in real time.
