## 2. ELEVATOR PITCH (30 sec)
Primordials is a desktop-native artificial life simulation inspired by the classic Lotka-Volterra predator-prey equations. It runs as a live wallpaper, screensaver, or standalone window, and visually depicts a real ecosystem where every organism births, hunts, flees, reproduces, and dies according to ecological rules. Built on a high-performance C++23 ECS engine and a modern React/TypeScript web layer.
