## 2. OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### Gensouls Lab Announces Primordials — An Ambient Digital Terrarium for Desktops

**BILASPUR, INDIA** — Gensouls Lab, an independent studio led by creator Sahil Khutey, has officially announced the launch of **Primordials**, a real-time artificial life simulation engineered to turn any computer screen into an active, evolving ecosystem.

Unlike traditional pre-rendered looping wallpapers or static screensavers, *Primordials* executes real-time mathematical biology. Organisms forage for energy, flee predators, mutate traits across generations, and establish dynamic equilibrium through continuous Lotka-Volterra differential equations.

*"Most digital backgrounds are decorative but lifeless,"* says developer Sahil Khutey. *"With Primordials, we wanted to build something that feels genuinely alive. Every creature has needs, behaviors, and genetic heritage. You can leave it running for hours in the background while you work or study, and return to find entirely new dominant species evolving."*

*Primordials* features six distinct biomes, including Deep Sea, Aurora, and Coral Reef, alongside custom color styling, chemical field diffusion, and zero-distraction ambient pacing.

The game and live wallpaper are available now on **itch.io**, **Wallpaper Engine Workshop**, and fully open-sourced on **GitHub**.
