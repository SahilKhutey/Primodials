## Phase Overview
- **Days 1–4:** Asset Generation & GitHub Hardening
- **Days 5–7:** Storefront Verification (itch.io, Wallpaper Engine)
- **Days 8–10:** Soft Launch & Creator Outreach
- **Days 11–14:** Full Public Launch & Multi-Channel Distribution
