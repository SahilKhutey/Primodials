# 🎨 PRIMODIALS — Brand Guide & Visual Identity System

## 1. BRAND ESSENCE
Primordials transforms static monitor displays into breathing digital terrariums.
**The Brand Promise:** Unobtrusive beauty, authentic mathematical biology, and deep tranquility.

### Core Character Attributes
- **Patient:** Ecosystems evolve with graceful pacing; never rushed or jarring.
- **Curious:** An invitation to tweak, observe, and discover emergent behaviors.
- **Scientific:** Rigorous Lotka-Volterra mechanics presented with accessible elegance.
- **Hypnotic:** Subtle ambient glows, particle motes, and bioluminescent trails.

## 4. LOGOMARK SPECIFICATIONS

### Visual Metaphor
The Primordials logomark represents the fundamental binary of ecology:
1. An illuminated cellular nucleus (the Prey/Life source — circle).
2. An orbiting, sharp geometric shard (the Predator/Evolutionary catalyst — dynamic triangle).
3. A subtle orbital ring denoting cyclic population balance.

### Clear Space & Minimum Sizing
- **Digital Favicon:** Minimum 16×16px / 32×32px
- **App Icon:** 512×512px SVG & PNG
- **Clearance:** 1.5× the height of the inner nucleus around all borders.

## 5. COPY & VOICE GUIDELINES

### Do Say:
- *"Watch life unfold on your desktop."*
- *"Real ecological dynamics, rendered at 60 FPS."*
- *"A window into an evolving digital terrarium."*
- *"Tune genetics, mutation rates, and chemical gradients."*

### Don't Say:
- *"Mindblowing next-gen AI graphics"* (Hyped and inaccurate)
- *"Ultra hardcore survival game"* (Misaligns with ambient wallpaper intent)
