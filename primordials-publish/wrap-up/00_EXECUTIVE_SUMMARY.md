# 🧬 PRIMORDIALS — One-Page Executive Summary

---

## 🎯 WHAT IT IS

> **Primordials is a desktop-native artificial life simulation that turns your monitor into a window into a living, evolving ecosystem.** Every organism obeys real Lotka-Volterra predator-prey differential dynamics. It runs as a live wallpaper, screensaver, or standalone laboratory window.

**Tagline:** *"A living ecosystem on your desktop."*  
**The Core Insight:** Screensavers and wallpapers have been static or looped video for 30 years. Primordials is the first ambient background that is truly alive, deterministic, and scientifically grounded.
