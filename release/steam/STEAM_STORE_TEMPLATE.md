# Polygonal Primordials — Steam Store Template

## Title
Polygonal Primordials

## Tagline
A living artificial ecosystem on your desktop.

## Short Description
Watch organisms move, reproduce, compete and evolve in a continuously changing artificial ecosystem.

## Feature Pillars

### Living ecosystem
Populations change through reproduction, survival and environmental pressure.

### Observe evolution
Inspect organisms and species and watch traits succeed or disappear.

### A world that keeps changing
Leave the simulation running and return later to see how the ecosystem developed.

### Desktop presentation
Use the living world as an immersive desktop experience.
