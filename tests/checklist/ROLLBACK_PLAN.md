# 🚨 Rollback & Hotfix Plan

## Incident Severities
- **SEV-1 (Critical):** Immediate hotfix or build revert within 2 hours.
- **SEV-2 (Major):** Hotfix within 24 hours.
- **SEV-3 (Minor):** Patch in upcoming minor release.

## Emergency Rollback Steps
1. Revert default branch to last stable tag on Steamworks.
2. Publish status update on Steam & Discord.
3. Deploy patch v2.0.1.
